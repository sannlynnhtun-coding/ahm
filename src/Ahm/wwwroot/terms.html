Using Ahm

Ahm is a free community tool that helps shops manage purchases, stamps, cashback and reward records in their own Google Sheet. It is not a payment processor or accounting system.

Shop owner responsibilities

The shop owner is responsible for Google Sheet sharing, obtaining any consent required to collect and use customer information, setting reward rules, correcting records and maintaining backups. Customers should contact their shop about loyalty records or rewards.

Staff access

Ahm treats every editor of a shop's Google Sheet as a trusted operator. Editors can also view and change records directly in Google Sheets, so shop owners should grant editing access only to trusted people.

Service availability

Ahm depends on Google APIs, Google Sheets and its hosting provider. Availability, quotas and features can change. Users remain responsible for reviewing their data and maintaining appropriate backups.
